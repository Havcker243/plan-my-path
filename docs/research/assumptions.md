# Site integration assumptions — October 7, 2026

- A13: The site is an experiment interface, not a new benchmark. Official setup stages, official evaluated tasks, and synthetic recovery trials retain distinct evidence scopes and comparison groups. No cross-suite ranking is valid. Revisit after adapting selective recovery to the same released tasks and evaluator as the baseline.
- A14: Initial research execution is local, Windows, single-worker, and restricted to verified Fisk login emails explicitly listed by the backend operator. One process-local lock serializes runs; persistent job files are not a distributed scheduler. Keep public execution disabled until a worker queue, durable storage, quotas and deployment dependencies are implemented.
- A15: Live site controls use one fixed Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite trial. This is a reproducibility/smoke-test choice, not a power analysis or paper-model reproduction. No statistical superiority claims follow. Official reward zero remains zero even when the process exits successfully.
## Official-task extension assumptions — October 7, 2026

- A16: Released tasks/evaluator plus researcher-defined faults are a controlled extension, not an official recovery benchmark or reproduction of named recovery papers. Full AgentRx is access-blocked; the custom adapter does not satisfy published-method reproduction.
- A17: Version-matched read re-execution assumes deterministic reads and trustworthy local tools/database. The detector excludes injected labels and expected actions. It cannot identify internally consistent wrong policies/database contents.
- A18: Conservative dependencies can make selective and source rollback equivalent. Single-tool calls may provide no independent cache work. The pilot's equal reward and greater selective work must be retained; do not tune selection to force an advantage.
- A19: v2 adds fixed six-second pacing after a v1 quota failure. All methods/participants receive the same delay within wall time. Keep versions/source hashes separate. The 171-cell matrix is planned, not completed.
- A20: Fisk 2024–2025 is explicitly selected, not assumed current/cohort-applicable. Catalog ambiguities and incomplete prerequisites/non-course approvals prevent graduation clearance. Synthetic elective credits do not prove requirement satisfaction. Production rules require review before replacement.
- A21: Saved-artifact comparisons require matching recorded protocol, adapter, configuration, input and upstream provenance. Exactly one attempt per strategy/task/fault/seed is required for the current paired-delta analysis; repeated ambiguous attempts are retained without selecting a favorable pair. Matching hashes do not control provider drift, hardware or all other unrecorded conditions. Null rewards and faults not exercised remain separate from graded recovery failures. Descriptive pilot comparisons do not establish statistical significance or causality.
- A22: The October 8 adapter adds monotonic tool-error accounting relative to the archived October 7 pilot. Matching protocol bytes do not make these source versions one comparison group. The four new live cells must not be pooled with the old seed-300 pair as three repeated matched seeds.
- A23: Filtering the frozen six-method rotation to reference/selective preserves method order but does not produce balanced ordering for seeds 301/302. Provider drift, shared quota use and method order remain possible confounders. Two quota errors leave zero eligible graded pairs in the new four-cell subset; future attempts require predeclared identities and pairing/exclusion rules while retaining all earlier failures.
- A24: A simulated user may emit STOP while confirming a required action, ending an official task before that write executes. A recorded correct source prediction therefore does not imply task recovery success. Inspect final traces and evaluator evidence without editing official user prompts, suppressing STOP, or retrospectively replacing the score. Offline scripted mechanism checks do not establish counterfactual live outcomes.
- A25: The fresh current-adapter seed-300 pair passed both methods with equal steps/model/tool counts and zero read-cache reuse. One pair's approximately 0.20-second duration difference does not establish a speed or work advantage; all earlier incomplete pairs remain visible. Equal calls can still differ in token usage/cost. Additional methods/tasks/faults and repetitions are needed before claiming a consistent benefit.
- A26: The 180-second upstream timeout is cooperative, not a hard cutoff. Full restart in campaign tau-matrix-20261009T050612Z-2fe24d earned reward 1.0 after approximately 196 seconds. Timeout checking excludes an already-done conversation; preserve that score while disclosing the budget overrun. Any stricter enforcement requires a separate versioned protocol. Overlapping workspace campaigns also prevent a claim of exclusive provider traffic; preserve duplicate attempts without favorable selection.
- A27: v3 reuses tool values only after selective recovery, exact request/version matching and independent canonical-response validation. This verifies deterministic tool values, not reasoning independence or semantic correctness of arguments. Source/error entries and writes are excluded; validation overhead counts toward total tool invocations. Positive cache reuse alone cannot establish net savings. Earlier argument-only and conservative-cache adapter results remain separate evidence groups; offline correctness tests do not establish live performance.
- A28: The completed AgentRx public-sample run had no ground-truth file supplied. Post-run inspection found released task-39 annotations in the pinned checkout; a separate frozen-prediction comparison matches root step 17 but mismatches the category. Two agreeing annotation files are copies of one case, not independent observations. No new model call or label-fed rerun was used. Prior benchmark exposure or upstream-example overlap is not excluded. Copilot auto does not establish a fixed model or reproduction of paper results; diagnosis agreement does not prove recovery efficacy.
