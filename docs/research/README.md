# FiskGrad Agent Research

This directory is the living record for the Senior Seminar II project.

- `literature-notes.md` records sources, findings, uncertainties, and the novelty audit.
- `project-draft.md` is the current research proposal and paper draft.
- `research-log.md` records dated decisions, searches, experiments, and next actions.
- `demo-plan.md` defines the staged demonstration, benchmark outputs, and evidence boundaries.
- `assumptions.md` records decisions, evidence, uncertainty, and revisit conditions.
- `baselines/README.md` tracks official method/benchmark reproduction, pinned sources, commands, and access requirements.

As of October 7, the primary evaluation direction is reproduction on released benchmarks before custom recovery development. Earlier custom-scenario plans are superseded as the primary evaluation; synthetic demos remain engineering evidence. See the dated research-log entry for completed checks, live successes/failures, and the reproduction transition.

Claims in the draft must be traceable to either a cited source, a reproducible experiment, or a clearly marked hypothesis. Synthetic prototype results are engineering checks, not research findings.
