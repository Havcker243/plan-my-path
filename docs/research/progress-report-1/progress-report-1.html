Progress Report 1

Selective State Recovery for Reliable AI Agents

Oludolapo Adegbesan
Case study: Fisk University B.S. in Computer Science

1. Project Overview

My project investigates how a tool-using AI agent can recover when an early mistake becomes visible later in a workflow. The central question is how to identify a safe recovery boundary, invalidate affected results, and preserve valid independent work. Academic planning is the intended case study because credits, grades and requirements provide opportunities for deterministic validation.

I am developing and evaluating a selective recovery prototype. Its effectiveness remains a research question. Published benchmark components provide a reference environment; custom recovery modifications are labeled separately.

2. Work Completed

I implemented a Python execution-trace and dependency layer, a deterministic recovery demonstration, and a live Gemini diagnosis harness. The synthetic comparison includes full restart, visible-step retry, simulated late-checkpoint restoration, source rollback, and selective recovery.

I integrated a Research Lab into the Next.js/FastAPI application. It launches fixed experiments and displays saved results, methods, traces, provenance and JSON exports. The interface separates official benchmarks, custom recovery on official tasks, synthetic demonstrations, and the Fisk catalog case study.

I pinned official AgentRx and maintained tau benchmark repositories, ran official preprocessing and environment checks, and executed a live released retail task. I then implemented an external controlled-fault recovery adapter using the released tools and unchanged evaluator. I also extracted Fisk catalog facts and created four synthetic academic audit cases.

Current status: a working research prototype with preliminary live evidence. Full published recovery-method reproduction, repeated experiments and complete Fisk graduation validation remain unfinished.

REFLECTION / NEXT RESEARCH STAGE

4. Challenges

Locating an earlier error. Retrying the visible failure can preserve corrupted state. The official-task adapter re-executes recorded reads against the same database version, finds the earliest content/error mismatch and invalidates its conservative dependency closure. Recovery restores conversation state and the isolated retail database together. This assumes deterministic trusted reads and does not establish a globally minimal recovery boundary.

Provider failures and fair comparison. Connection and quota errors interrupted early attempts. I preserved those failures and recorded a versioned amendment adding equal generation pacing to both strategies. Global attempt, time and model-usage counts include discarded work.

Access and academic ground truth. Full AgentRx diagnosis requires supported provider access. I have not claimed end-to-end reproduction. Catalog ambiguities remain flagged rather than permitting graduation clearance. Local frontend/API integration issues were addressed; browser connection problems still prevent a claim of completed visual verification.
